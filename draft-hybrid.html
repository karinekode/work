Project · L’Oréal

Enterprise GenAI Assistant

An agentic RAG and MCP assistant that gives employees cited answers from internal knowledge, in 26 markets.

1,200+active users in 3 months
92%answer relevance
−16%infrastructure cost
−67%LLM context size

Problem

Knowledge lived across SharePoint, wikis and internal platforms, and teams in different markets relied on specific people to find it. A first RAG pipeline over about 3,000 documents hit three limits:

  • Poor retrieval: irrelevant chunks surfaced from unrelated departments.
  • Fragmented sources: content was spread across several platforms and went stale.
  • Limited reach: plain RAG could search documents but not call live systems or structured data.

Approach

I moved to an agentic architecture of scoped sub-agents, each with its own retrieval strategy, failure modes and handoff protocol. MCP gave every agent a standard way to call tools such as the Elium API, which made the system far easier to extend than ad-hoc API wiring.

Fine-tuning made responses consistently carry citations to official sources.

Evaluation

I combined human review with an LLM-as-a-judge. From 100 real user queries I generated 500 synthetic Q&A cases. A sample was human-annotated for accuracy, relevance and tone, and 70% of it calibrated the judge through disagreement analysis. I validated the judge on the held-out set using Spearman correlation, exact agreement and within-one-point agreement, then deployed it on the remaining 400 cases.

Cost and latency

  • Semantic caching: frequent queries match a pre-built Q&A store in BigQuery and skip the LLM.
  • Cross-encoder re-ranking: 30 retrieved chunks (about 15K tokens) are cut to the top 10, a 67% smaller context.
  • Response streaming: tokens stream in real time, so the tool feels fast and trustworthy.

Project · L’Oréal

AI/ML Demand Forecasting

A forecasting platform that replaced spreadsheets and gut feel, and won the L’Oréal Global Tech Blazer Award.

$4.5Mannual cost savings
−35%excess inventory
−42%airfreight costs
24brands, 5 countries

Problem

Demand planners forecast hundreds of SKUs with Excel and personal judgment, reaching only 50–70% accuracy. The slow, inconsistent process led to excess inventory or missed sales.

Approach

SKU behaviour varies widely, so instead of forcing one model I used an ensemble of two model families.

LightGBM for stable, high-volume SKUs. It retrains weekly, keeps forecasts fresh without heavy compute, and comes with SHAP explanations. When a stakeholder asks why a spike is coming, there is a clear visual answer.

Temporal Fusion Transformer for new launches and long-tail SKUs. With little or no history, such as holiday gift sets, the TFT detects seasonality, takes future inputs like promotional spend, and outputs confidence intervals so planners see a range of likely futures.

A data-quality tab flags missing inputs before they hurt a forecast.

Production

The full lifecycle ran on Vertex AI: feature engineering, tuning, evaluation and drift monitoring, with MLflow for experiment tracking.

Project · Johnson & Johnson

Next-Best-Action Recommendation Engine

A personalised engine that tells each sales rep who to contact, with what content, through which channel, and how often.

$9Msales uplift
12%YoY growth
70K+recommendations a month
5APAC markets

Problem

“Who do I call? What do I say? How often? Through which channel?”

With 300+ sales reps and 10,000+ healthcare professionals across APAC, reps drifted toward the same familiar doctors, content and habits, leaving sales uplift on the table. The decisions were too complex to optimise by hand.

Approach

For every rep × healthcare professional pair, the model scored each viable strategy (channel, content, contact frequency) by expected sales uplift and surfaced the best one inside the commercial workflow.

  • Embedding layers learned dense representations of each rep and HCP, avoiding a huge sparse one-hot space.
  • Behavioural and engagement features added context for each interaction.
  • MLP layers modelled interactions between rep, HCP and strategy.
  • A regression head predicted expected sales uplift per recommendation.

Engineering

The harder part was fitting into existing commercial workflows: reliable, low-latency recommendations at scale, deployed with Docker on AWS EKS and tracked with MLflow.
